Hey @looker_dakidaki, thanks for the detailed report, and sorry for the confusion here.

Your Agent Computer is healthy and nothing is stuck on our side. Your bots, conversations, configurations and computer data are all intact, and the reset you ran did not remove anything. Please don’t reset again.

What actually happened is that your account reached its weekly included Grok Bot usage limit. Since then every message you send is being declined, and the app should be showing you a clear notice about that instead of just staying silent. It isn’t, and we’re fixing that. The usage you see as available on cursor.com is your Cursor plan usage, which is a separate pool from Grok Bot usage. Grok Bot Settings → Usage is the place to check the Grok Bot pool.

If you’d like to keep working before then, you can turn on on-demand usage for this same account at cursor.com/dashboard, which lets Grok Bot continue past the included amount.
