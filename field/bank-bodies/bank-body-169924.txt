## Post #2 @deanrie (2026-08-29T15:53:48.317Z)
Hey, thanks for the report. What you’re describing matches an issue we’re tracking. The helper that connects Grok Bot to your local computer starts, but doesn’t finish registration, so chat keeps working but local computer access stays unavailable. I’ve shared your case with the team. A reset like this usually helps: Fully quit Grok Bot. Right-click the tray icon and click Quit, not just closing the window. Open Task Manager and end any remaining Grok Bot processes. Launch Grok Bot again and give it a couple minutes to reconnect. If your local computer still doesn’t show up, sign out and sign back in in Grok Bot. That fully recreates the helper. Version 0.31.0 is also rolling out now, so it’s worth checking for an update and upgrading. This isn’t related to your subscription. On our side it’s active and looks fine. This is specifically about the helper reconnecting after an update. Let me know how it goes. If it still doesn’t help, we’ll dig deeper.
