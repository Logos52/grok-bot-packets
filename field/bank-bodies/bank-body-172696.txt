## Post #5 @deanrie (2026-09-23T04:33:16.746Z)
Hey, thanks for the detailed report and the screenshots. Good news first. The Grok Bot computer on our side is working fine. Each Recover and Reset did create a new computer, and it came up in about a minute. The problem is that your Mac’s network can’t resolve the address the app uses to connect to it. This lookup happens on your side, so Recover and Reset won’t fix it. For now, it’s best not to press Reset again. Try switching your Mac to public DNS: System Settings → Network. Select your active connection (Wi-Fi or Ethernet) → Details. DNS tab. Under DNS Servers, click + and add 1.1.1.1 , then click + again and add 8.8.8.8 . OK. Fully quit Grok Bot (menu bar icon → Quit, or Cmd+Q ). Just closing the window isn’t enough. Open Grok Bot again and wait about 2 minutes. If you want to test the lookup directly, open Terminal and run: nslookup test.us12.cursorvm.com nslookup test.us12.cursorvm.com 1.1.1.1 If the first command fails but the second returns an address, that confirms it’s a DNS issue. If you can’t change DNS, switching to a different ISP can help. A hotspot from the same provider usually won’t help. After switching networks, fully quit and reopen the app again. Let me know if the Can’t reach your computer banner disappears after step 7. If not, please send the output of both commands.
