Hey, thanks for the detailed report. I’ll go through it point by point. On Finance/Plaid: This connector isn’t released to users yet, so you can’t install or connect it on your account right now. The listing your bot found, and the “Added finance” message, are part of unreleased work that leaked into the UI. That’s why the Plaid Link window didn’t show up, and the server didn’t start in GetMcpServerStatus. I shared your interest with the team, but I can’t share dates yet. On signing in to bank sites: Many banks treat logins coming from the bot’s cloud environment as a new, unknown device and they block or delay the sign-in. That’s where the “temporary delays” on Citi come from. So signing into each bank through the bot browser isn’t a working approach right now. On SendFeedback: The tool really is unavailable while Privacy Mode is enabled. That’s expected behavior, nothing is broken. Posting here was the right move. For the chat sync issue, I need a bit more info. Please try this: On your computer, open the chat that looks different and send a short message like “sync test”. On your iPhone, fully close the Grok Bot app. Open the app again and open the same chat. Check whether “sync test” and the earlier messages show up. If not, send the bot name, the app version on your computer and on your iPhone, and a screenshot of the same chat on both devices. Let me know how it goes.
