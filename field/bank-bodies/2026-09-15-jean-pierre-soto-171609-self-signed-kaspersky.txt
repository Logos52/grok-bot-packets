Update / resolved

The issue was caused by Kaspersky HTTPS scanning (SSL/TLS inspection), not by Grok Bot or my Cursor account.

Kaspersky intercepts HTTPS traffic and injects its own certificate into the chain. Network Debugger then reports:

API TLS: TLS certificate untrusted: SELF_SIGNED_CERT_IN_CHAIN

After that, Auth, Updates, Feature flags, Origin API, Telemetry, Voice realtime, and *.cursorvm.com all failed. DNS still worked because it isn’t HTTPS.

Fix: disable Kaspersky HTTPS/SSL scanning (or exclude Cursor / Grok Bot), or briefly use another network without that interception. After that, Grok Bot reconnects normally.

Hope this helps anyone hitting the same SELF_SIGNED_CERT_IN_CHAIN error.
