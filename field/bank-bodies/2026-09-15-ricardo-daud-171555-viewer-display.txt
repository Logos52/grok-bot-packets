--- @deanrie 2026-09-14T14:56:13.789Z (#7) ---
Thanks for the screenshot, that’s the key detail. What you’re seeing isn’t a viewer bug or a black screen. The viewer is connected and is rendering the bot machine’s desktop (gray wallpaper, Chrome and a terminal at the bottom). So the live screen connection from your Mac is working fine.

The problem is elsewhere. The IBKR Gateway window is not on the desktop that Take over is showing.

From your description, the bot started a separate stack (Xvfb :1 → x11vnc 127.0.0.1:5900 → noVNC :6080) and launched IBKR on DISPLAY=:1. But Take over is showing the machine’s main desktop, the one in the screenshot. These are two different displays. The bot “sees” its window on :1, but in the viewer you’re looking at an empty main desktop. That’s why the bot screenshot and the live viewer don’t match.

What to try:

Ask the bot to open something visible (for example, a terminal) on the main desktop, the one shown in Take over. If it appears in the viewer, you’re finally looking at the same display.

Then ask the bot to start IBKR Gateway on that same main display, without its own Xvfb :1 / x11vnc / noVNC.

After that, the IBKR window should show up in Take over, and you can finish the Paper login using your keyboard.

Based on the screenshot, the network side already looks fine, so you can pause the hotspot steps for now. This really looks like a display issue where IBKR is being launched. Let me know what happens.
