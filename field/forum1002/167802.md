## @kevinn 2026-08-10T21:07:45.577Z STAFF
Hi @Stanislav_Chistyakov Sorry to hear this. We don’t have immediate plans to add additional limits like weekly guardrails (in fact, the flexibility of the Cursor plan is one of the important product diffentiators and features that people enjoy, .e.g if you want to have a crazy weekend and use most of your plan in one weekend during a hackathon- you can!)

The best ways to keep usage tracked are as follows:

Usage summary under the prompt. In Cursor Settings, search for Usage Summary (under Agents) and set it to Always. The default, Auto, only appears when you are already close to the limit. In the Agents Window, that readout sits in the status area right below the prompt. The same setting also controls the bar at the bottom of the chat pane in the classic IDE. If you see the Local / context row there instead of plan usage, turn off View > Status Bar so the usage summary can show.

Dashboard. cursor.com/dashboard/spending and cursor.com/dashboard/usage show remaining allowance in both pools (Cursor Models and Other Models), plus a per-day / per-model breakdown that can help explain a sudden jump.

As you know, Ultra has two separate monthly pools. If any Cursor Models usage is still left, Composer 2.5 and Grok 4.5 can keep you building without turning on on-demand.

Additionally, while account sharing is prohibited, there is no policy that prevents you from having two ultra accounts, so if you’d like to sign up for another, that may be a potential avenue for you.
