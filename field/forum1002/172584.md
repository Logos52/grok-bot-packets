## @mohitjain 2026-09-22T06:57:45.340Z STAFF
Hey @Zachariah, there’s no tier above Ultra for Grok Bot, and Cursor and SuperGrok pools don’t stack, so there’s no “Ultra Ultra” to buy. I’ve noted the demand for a bigger tier for the team.

On gauging cost: we don’t publish a dollar figure for the weekly included allowance, and there’s no fixed “$X = N requests,” since Grok Bot bills by the actual tokens and steps a task uses, not by messages. The real gauge is on-demand itself. Turn it on with a modest monthly cap at cursor.com/dashboard → Spending, and you’ll see your actual per-task cost in dollars in real time, with a ceiling so there are no surprises.

To stretch the weekly pool: start a fresh chat per task (the + at the top of the chat list), and keep delegating heavy execution to Cloud Workers, which bill against your Cursor plan usage rather than the Grok Bot weekly pool.

More: Grok Bot plans and billing. Other Ultra users comparing setups: making the weekly allowance last.
