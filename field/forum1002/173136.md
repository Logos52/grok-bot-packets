## @mohitjain 2026-09-28T08:29:24.969Z STAFF
Hey @Ahmed_Elkiki,

Your bots, files and logins are safe on your computer, and it’s running fine on our side, so please don’t run Reset again. The issue is that since the update, the app on this PC can’t connect out to our servers (that’s why Retry, Recover, Reset and Send Feedback all fail, and why signing out left a “?”). This is almost always security software intercepting the connection.

In a browser, open https://api2.cursor.sh/, click the padlock, and check “Issued by”. It should say Amazon. If it names your antivirus (Kaspersky, ESET, Avast, Bitdefender, Norton), that’s the cause.
In that antivirus, add Grok Bot as a trusted app with HTTPS / “encrypted connection” scanning turned off for it (just pausing protection often isn’t enough). If you use a VPN or proxy, pause it.
Fully quit Grok Bot from the tray, reopen, and give it a couple of minutes. (Still stuck? Try once on a phone hotspot with VPN/proxy off.)

If none of that helps, reinstall the latest from https://x.ai/bot. Your bots and files live on the computer, so it won’t touch them.

Then let me know what the padlock showed and which antivirus/VPN you use. You shouldn’t be hitting this and it’s not something you misconfigured; it’s an issue we’re tracking.
