Hey, thanks for the report, and sorry about the routing confusion. Your post was automatically sent to the billing thread at first, but it’s a product report. We’ve moved it to the right category, so the team will see it.

Here’s a quick breakdown:

Google login inside the Grok Bot browser. What you’re seeing is a known limitation, not something in your setup. Login sharing works reliably for sites that keep sessions in cookies. Google and many app-style sites store part of the session elsewhere in the browser like localStorage or IndexedDB. Because of that, signing in inside the agent browser may fail or ask you to sign in again, and the session can drop after the computer is recreated. The team is working on improvements here, but I can’t share a timeline yet.

Import cookies from Chrome. You don’t see this option because it’s not broadly available yet. It’s the feature that should let you bring your local Chrome logins into the agent computer, and it’s still in development. I’ll reply here once there’s something you can try.

Weekly usage showing no numbers. We’ll need a bit more info to debug this. Please share a screenshot of the dashboard where usage shows without numbers, plus your Cursor and Grok Bot versions from Settings. Then we can check what exactly isn’t showing.

Related thread about logins in the agent browser, if you’re curious: Grok Bot: per-agent browser still requires separate sign-in despite account-level login promise

Let me know about the usage issue, and feel free to post again if anything else comes up.
