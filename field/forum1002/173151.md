## @mohitjain 2026-09-28T08:26:12.450Z STAFF
Hey @Ted_Glenwright! You can definitely add more than one - each bank just goes through its own Plaid session.

On the Finance plugin page (where Chase shows), look for Add Account below your linked accounts and tap it to open Plaid and pick Charles Schwab. Reinstalling won’t remove Chase, so no need to reset - your connections live with your Finance account, not the plugin.

If Add Account doesn’t open Plaid, update to the latest version, fully close and reopen the app, and try again. Still stuck? Reply with a screenshot and your app version and I’ll dig in.

Linking several banks in one Plaid session isn’t available yet, but I’ve passed it along.

## @Jason_Chollar 2026-09-30T20:13:40.089Z
What worked more smoothly for me: set up a free account at https://my.plaid.com/ first and link all your banks there. Then, when you connect the Finance plugin, sign in with that same Plaid account so your institutions are ready to share. It saves reopening Plaid for each bank one at a time.
