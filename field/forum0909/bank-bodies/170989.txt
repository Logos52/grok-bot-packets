## deanrie — 2026-09-08T07:08:04.844Z
Hey, thanks for the detailed report, your breakdown really helps. What you described isn’t related to your settings. It’s the result of a service issue, not anything you did. What happened: on 7 September at around 17:00 UTC, there was an incident that caused a second copy of Agent Computer to appear for your account. Both copies kept running your scheduled tasks. That’s why you saw different results on macOS and Android and the chat got out of sync. You read it correctly. Right now, both Mac and Android are connected to the same copy, the one your Mac switched to after the restart. So what you’re seeing today is consistent. Important: while we’re investigating, please don’t click Reset, Recover, or Update Computer. That could create another copy. About possible double usage due to duplicate runs, please email hi@cursor.com and the team can check your account. We’re tracking this issue. I’ll post here if there’s an update.
