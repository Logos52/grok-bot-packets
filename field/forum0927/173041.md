## Post 5 @deanrie [staff] · 2026-09-26T09:24:07.167Z
Hey, thanks for the report and screenshots. I can see both screens, Can’t reach your computer and the cover page with *.cursorvm.com / Zscaler.

Good news, your Grok Bot computer on our side is working fine. Your Bots, files, and logins are all still there. The issue is that the Grok Bot app on your PC can’t reach our servers at all right now. Neither Retry nor Recover is leaving the machine, so it can’t upload anything. No need to keep clicking Recover, it won’t change anything from here. Also, the *.cursorvm.com mention in the error is misleading. The real problem is that the app can’t establish a connection.

Your app version is also very old, and it couldn’t auto-update. The updater uses the same blocked channel. So let’s start with a clean reinstall.

Right-click the Grok Bot icon in the system tray bottom-right near the clock and click Quit. Then open Task Manager and make sure there are no Grok Bot processes left.

Download the latest installer from 
Cursor · Download Grok Bot
 and run it.

Open Grok Bot, sign in with the same account, and give it about 2 minutes.

If it still can’t reach the computer:

If you have an antivirus or security app with web protection or HTTPS / encrypted connection scanning enabled, for example Kaspersky, Quick Heal, ESET, Avast, AVG, Norton, Bitdefender, add Grok Bot to exclusions or temporarily disable that scanning. Then quit and reopen Grok Bot.

Open Command Prompt, run 
curl.exe -sI https://api2.cursor.sh
, and paste the output here.

Quick test: connect your PC to your phone hotspot, quit and reopen Grok Bot, wait 2 minutes, and tell me if it loads.

Also, do your Bots show up right now in the Grok Bot Android app? That’ll help narrow down the cause.
