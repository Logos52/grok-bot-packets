## Post 6 @Colin [staff] · 2026-09-26T14:55:48.081Z
Hey 
@Gurkirat_Singh
, thanks for the detailed report, and for sharing the workaround.

You’re right about the cause. Grok Bot currently checks server certificates against its own built-in list of public certificate authorities rather than the Windows certificate store, so when Kaspersky replaces the certificate for its encrypted-connection scanning, Grok Bot rejects the connection. The browser, curl, and Cursor all use the Windows store, which is why they keep working.

Adding Grok Bot.exe as a trusted application with “Do not scan all traffic” (or turning off encrypted-connection scanning) is the right fix for now, so please keep that exclusion in place.

I’ve passed this to the team, including both points you raised: the error should say the certificate isn’t trusted and name the issuer, and the app shouldn’t keep retrying and opening new connections when this happens.
