## Post 10 @deanrie [staff] · 2026-09-26T13:38:39.347Z
Hey, thanks for sending the screenshot of the table, that helps.

About the image, the limit here isn’t the file size, it’s the image dimensions in pixels. Table screenshots often end up very wide, several thousand pixels across, even if they look like a thin strip. Because of that, one of the images in this chat history is over the maximum allowed size, and every new request in this chat hits that limit, so even “hello” fails. Update and Reset don’t help because this chat’s history is stored on our side.

On timing, I honestly can’t give an exact date. I’ve passed this to the team and we’ll reply here as soon as we have an update.

Waiting is also totally fine. Your bots and all history are safe, nothing is being deleted. The only downside is that this specific chat will stay stuck for now. If you want to use Grok Bot in the meantime, the fastest option is to start a new chat, you can use the same bot, just without that wide image. You don’t need to touch the old chat.
