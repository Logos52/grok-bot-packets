## @deanrie STAFF · 2026-09-30T16:48:01
Hey, thanks for the report. What you’re seeing is that Grok Bot gets to the password step in the Google popup and can’t fill it from 1Password. This is an issue we’re tracking. I shared your report with the team. For now, the workaround is to sign in to Google manually once on the bot screen: When the bot hands you control of the screen, type the password yourself from your Google item in 1Password. Complete 2-step verification if Google asks. Hand control back to the bot. The bot’s browser usually keeps that Google session, so the next Sign in with Google should only ask you to pick an account, without the password step.
