## @kevinn STAFF · 2026-09-30T19:52:35
Hey @Kevin_Carroll , thanks for the report. Around 10 AM ET, Asana stopped accepting the saved sign-in for your Asana connector, which is why all three accounts switched to needing authentication together. The connector itself is still saved on your account. It just drops out of the agent’s connector list until it is signed in again. The redirect_uri error comes from Asana. It means the Asana app your connector is configured with (the one whose Client ID was entered when the connector was set up) doesn’t list the callback address Grok Bot uses for sign-in. Could you try this: Open the Asana developer console at Log in - Asana and select that app. Open its OAuth settings and find Redirect URLs. Add Cursor Agent - Turn your ideas into code and save. Leave any existing URLs in place. In Grok Bot, start the Asana sign-in again for the default account. Once that works, repeat the sign-in for “cos” and “rls”. If Asana still shows the redirect_uri error after step 4, reply with a screenshot of the app’s Redirect URLs list and the time you tried, and we’ll dig in further.
