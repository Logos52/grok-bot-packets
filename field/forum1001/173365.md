## @deanrie STAFF · 2026-09-30T05:06:22
Hey, thanks for the detailed report and the screenshot. I can see two Filled cards in the bot chat, followed by diagnostics. What you’re describing is a known issue we’re tracking. On some login pages, including Instagram and Letterboxd, the page won’t accept the password that 1Password auto-fills. After that, Grok Bot clears the password field for security. The card still shows Filled even though the login didn’t actually go through. That’s why you see the username filled but the password field empty. This isn’t something in your setup. The password in your vault is fine, as you noticed. I can’t share a timeline yet, but I’ll reply in the thread when there’s an update. For now, login should work using either of these options: Ask the bot to use the secure in-chat login form for Instagram instead of 1Password. You enter the password directly in the chat form, and the bot can’t see it. Or take over the bot’s computer on the Instagram login page and log in yourself once. Enter the verification code there too if Instagram asks for it. After that, the bot’s browser should stay logged in. Let me know if either option doesn’t work.
