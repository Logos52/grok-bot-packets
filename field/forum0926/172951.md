## Colin @ 2026-09-25T04:01:14.511Z
Hey @sd-tenbridge , thanks for the report! This is a known issue we’re already tracking, and I’ve added your report to it. On the mobile app, some bots don’t pick up the voice and speed you saved, so each new call starts on the default voice. Changes made during a call also only last for that call. Your saved Sal @ 1.25x setting is intact, which is why the desktop app still uses it. In the meantime, you can switch the voice back to Sal and the speed to 1.25x from the settings (gear) button inside the call. That will hold for the rest of that call. Will share updates on this thread!
