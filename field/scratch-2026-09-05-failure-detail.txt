@Colin: Hey @LLMTester , thanks for the detailed report. What you are seeing is how a Grok Bot agent works today, rather than something broken in your setup! Each bot has one conversation and one memory, and that single history spans both its 1:1 chat with you and every group it is a member of. Group turns are tagged internally so the bot knows which room it is speaking in, but it is expected to draw on everything it knows, including its 1:1 history, and its saved memories belong to the bot, not to a chat. That means two long running projects on the same bot will cross over, exactly as you describe. The reliable way to keep Project X and Project Y separate right now is to give each project its own bot.
