## #6 mohitjain [STAFF] · 2026-08-12T11:08:52Z
Hey @yu-iskw The short version: when you use Grok Bot through your Cursor account (Ultra or Team Premium), the data handling follows your Cursor account and privacy settings, not xAI’s general privacy policy. The generic x.ai/legal pages you linked cover xAI’s own products, which is why they don’t say anything Cursor-specific. A few specifics: Cursor Privacy Mode does apply to Grok Bot. With Privacy Mode on, your Grok Bot data isn’t used for training by Cursor or any model provider (including xAI). For teams, the team’s privacy setting governs all members. Training opt-out follows your Cursor privacy settings, the same controls you already use across the rest of Cursor. One exception: Grok Bot needs data storage to run, so Legacy Privacy Mode (no storage) blocks Grok Bot entirely. If you’re on Legacy Privacy Mode, you’ll see a prompt about that. The Grok Bot docs cover this here: Approvals, security, and privacy , and the contractual details live in the Cursor Privacy Policy and security page .
