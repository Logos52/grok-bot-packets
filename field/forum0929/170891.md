## #6 Colin [STAFF] · 2026-09-07T12:54:34Z
Hey @jsolly , thanks for the report. This is a known issue we’re already tracking, and I’ve added your report to it. Right now Ahrefs’ service is rate limiting the connection attempts that Grok Bot makes on your behalf, so the connector never finishes loading and shows “Failed to load connector” with no tools. It is not something you can fix from your side, and reinstalling the plugin or re-adding the account won’t help. We’re looking at changing how Grok Bot connects to Ahrefs so it backs off and retries instead of failing, and I’ll update this thread as things develop! In the meantime, the connector may occasionally load if you retry later, but it will be unreliable until the change is out.
