## #5 deanrie [STAFF] · 2026-09-19T16:16:15Z
Hey, thanks for the report. Good news: it’s not an issue with your setup or with Chief of Staff itself, so there’s no need to reset, recreate, or delete anything. This was a server-side issue on our end. One turn for that bot got stuck, and the messages you sent after that weren’t lost, they were queued up. That’s what showed up as “Failed to send”. Looks like the bot has already unstuck itself and is replying normally again, and the message queue has been processed. The junior bots weren’t affected, since each bot runs as a separate worker. We’re tracking this issue. If a bot goes silent again, it usually recovers on its own within about 30 minutes. If it stays silent for more than about 45 minutes after your last message, reply here with the bot’s name and the time you sent the message, and we’ll take a look.
