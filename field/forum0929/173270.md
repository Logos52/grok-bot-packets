## #1 Jason_Chollar · 2026-09-28T21:20:47Z
Feature request for product/service – Other – Describe the request I’d love a built-in way, like a first-party Grok Bot connector, for my bots to read my own usage: how much of my weekly allowance is left, and how much on-demand I’ve used. Why: last weekend I ran several research bots at once and burned through my whole weekly allowance, then $8 of my $10 on-demand cushion, with no warning. Right now the only view is the Usage page in Settings, and the bots can’t see it at all. What would help: Bots can report my remaining allowance when I ask. Before a big job like deep research, a bot gives a rough estimate (“this will likely use about 20% of what’s left”) so I can decide first. An optional warning when I’m close to spilling into on-demand. Related: Weekly usage spills into On-Demand with no warning Anyone else want this?
