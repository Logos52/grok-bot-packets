## #5 deanrie [STAFF] · 2026-09-18T07:45:25Z
Hey, thanks for the detailed report. I looked into what’s happening, and the good news is your bots and Agent Computer are fine. Nothing is stuck. This is about the usage credit that comes with the Grok Bot free trial. It was fully used up on 16 September at around 20:45 UK time. From that point on, every message gets rejected before the bot can respond, which matches exactly when things went quiet. The app should’ve shown a clear “usage limit reached” notice instead of staying silent. It didn’t, and that’s a known issue we’re tracking. I’ve shared the details with the team. One more thing. In Settings > Usage, the percentage shows how much of the trial usage you’ve already used. So 100% means it’s all used up, not that it’s all available. To keep using the bots, you’ll need to upgrade to a Cursor plan that includes Grok Bot (upgrade options are shown in the Grok Bot app under Settings > Usage), or link a SuperGrok subscription. All your bots, chats, routines, and settings are saved and will continue from where you left off. You don’t need to reset or recover Agent Computer again. Let me know if anything behaves oddly after you upgrade.

## #9 deanrie [STAFF] · 2026-09-18T13:11:48Z
Hey, I get it. When a bot silently stops replying and gives you no signal at all, it’s really confusing and gets in the way. In most cases, this kind of “silent” stop means you’ve hit the usage limit. Your messages are sent, but they get rejected on the server before the bot can reply. The problem is that right now you don’t see a clear “usage limit reached” notification. It should show up, but it doesn’t. This is a known issue we’re tracking. You can check your limit in Settings > Usage the percentage shows how much you’ve used, not how much you have left. But in your case it might not be the limit. To look into your specific situation, can you share a couple of details: the name of the bot that went silent, and roughly when it last happened including your timezone the Request ID if you can grab it in the bot chat: menu in the top-right corner > Copy Request ID your Grok Bot version and OS version With that, I can check what’s happening on your side.

Hey Charles, glad you got a SuperGrok subscription, and sorry it’s been a frustrating day getting it connected. A SuperGrok link applies to whichever Cursor account (identity or email) it’s attached to.
