Hi @jsolly , thanks for the report! That error page is served by Todoist’s authorization server, not by Grok Bot. On our side, the Todoist sign-in link we generate always includes the PKCE code_challenge , including on the attempt that failed. So it looks like the parameter is being dropped somewhere between opening the page and reaching Todoist’s authorize endpoint. My best guess: it happens when Todoist first needs to sign you in (or refresh its web session) and then returns to the authorization page without those parameters. That would explain why the immediate retry succeeded. Does the error only occur on the attempt where Todoist asks you to sign in? Does the retry go straight to the permission screen?
