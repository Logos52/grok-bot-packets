## #6 deanrie [STAFF] · 2026-09-18T09:40:00Z
Hey, thanks for the detailed report. Your Agent Computer is fine, so there’s no need to Update, Recover, or Reset. The issue isn’t with it or your setup. What you’re seeing DM Failed to send, the @mention in the X运营 group appearing and disappearing while the Computer is still accessible is a known service-side issue that affects Grok Bot message processing. Your messages are still accepted and queued, they aren’t lost, and they should be processed once the bot is free. The message disappearing in the group is related to the same thing the group only waits for a bot reply for a limited time, and it stops waiting if the bot doesn’t respond in time. A couple notes for the future: Don’t create extra bots or do a Reset, it won’t help, and new bots can end up in the same situation. We’re tracking this issue, but I can’t share an exact timeline yet. If the bot stays silent for a long time, reply here with the bot name and the time of the last message, and we’ll take another look.
