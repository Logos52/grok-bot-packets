## #5 Colin [STAFF] · 2026-09-20T08:41:56Z
Hey @jsolly , thanks for the report, and for the screenshot! It would help a lot to know whether this happens every time or was a one-off. Could you try these steps? Force-quit Grok Bot from the app switcher and reopen it. Go to Plugins, open Your plugins, and tap Vercel. Tap the … menu in the top right of the sheet and choose Uninstall, then confirm. Stay on that same sheet and tap Add again. Wait about 5 seconds after the button changes to “Added” without tapping anything. Does the sheet close on its own after step 5? If it gets stuck again, please reply with your exact iOS version (Settings > General > About) and whether the sheet also gets stuck when you add a plugin that was not just uninstalled (for example, uninstall Vercel, close the sheet, then open it again and tap Add). In the meantime, force-quitting and reopening the app is the way out if it happens again; the plugin itself is installed correctly on our side.
