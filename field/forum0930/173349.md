## @pdfer · 2026-09-29T17:49:53.250Z

Describe the request The “Route traffic through this computer” setting is all-or-nothing today. When it’s on, every new browser connection from my Grok Bot’s computer goes out through my home machine. I’d like to choose this per site. A few sites, like Reddit signup and login, block data-center addresses. Everything else, like email, dashboards, and my own website, works fine over the cloud connection. This matters because my home machine is on Starlink in SW Florida. Storms can cut the connection or the power without warning, and the Grok Bot app doesn’t restart on its own after a reboot. With all traffic routed, one outage stalls every browser task until I’m back at the machine. What I’m asking for: A per-site list (domains) that goes through my computer, with everything else staying on the cloud path. Optionally, an app setting to launch at login so routing resumes after a reboot. Thanks!
