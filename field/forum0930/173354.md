## @kevinn [STAFF] · 2026-09-29T18:32:35.374Z

Hey @Emanuelle , you can skip 1Password. Grok Bot doesn’t need it to sign in to sites, and it’s the only password manager Grok Bot connects to today. When a Bot reaches a login, it hands you the computer. Open the computer, take over, type the password yourself (you can look it up in the Passwords app on your iPhone), and hand control back. The Bot doesn’t see the password, and the session stays signed in for later tasks, so you’ll only need to do this again if a site signs you out. You can also take over from the Grok Bot iPhone app. The service account step is only needed if you want the Bot to fill saved logins without you. Unless you run Grok Bot on a Mac with the 1Password app, which sets it up for you, that means creating a service account in 1Password and pasting its token, which is the step you’ve been stuck on. Since that also means paying for 1Password, I’d skip it and use the takeover above. Let me know any follow-up questions!
