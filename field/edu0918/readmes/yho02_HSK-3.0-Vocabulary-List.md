# HSK 3.0 Vocabulary with English Translations

Official HSK 3.0 vocabulary list (levels 1–6, 7-9), parsed from CTI's PDF syllabus and
merged with English translations. Built for personal study / Anki import; shared in
case it's useful to others.

This is **not** an official CTI/Hanban product and is **not** affiliated with or
endorsed by them. HSK (汉语水平考试) is their exam; this repo only contains data
extracted and reorganized from their public syllabus, plus community translation
data (see Sources below).

## Columns

- **level**: HSK 3.0 level — `1`, `2`, `3`, `4`, `5`, `6`, or `7-9` for the
  combined advanced tier (HSK 3.0 does not split 7-9 further).
- **word**: term in simplified characters, as listed in the official syllabus.
- **pinyin**: pinyin with tone diacritics, as listed in the official syllabus.
- **english**: English gloss(es), semicolon-separated when a term has more than
  one sense under the same pinyin reading. Matched against the translation
  source below by word + pinyin; **not proofread by a human** — treat as a
  starting point, not a dictionary-quality translation.

## Sources

- **Word list, pinyin, levels**: CTI (Chinese Test International) / Hanban,
  official HSK 3.0 Examination Syllabus (新版HSK考试大纲), published at
  https://www.chinesetest.cn/hsk. All rights to the original syllabus remain
  with CTI/Hanban; this repo republishes only the extracted word/level/pinyin
  data, not the original document, its layout, or its branding.
- **English translations**: [drkameleon/complete-hsk-vocabulary](https://github.com/drkameleon/complete-hsk-vocabulary)
  (MIT licensed), itself compiled from CC-CEDICT and other open sources.
  Not every term has a corresponding entry there
