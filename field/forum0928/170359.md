## Post 5 @deanrie · 2026-09-04T10:20:55.097Z
Hey, thanks for the feature request.
Right now, Grok Bot doesn’t have a first-party Plaid connector, and the Plaid connection from the Grok app doesn’t carry over to Grok Bot. They’re separate integrations. I passed this on to the team as a feature request. I can’t share a timeline yet, but if there’s an update, I’ll reply in the thread.
For now, connectors in Grok Bot are added via the Marketplace: Settings > Plugins. If your financial service is in the catalog, you can add it there and authorize it in your browser. More details here: Connect plugins | Cursor Docs
If you find a suitable read-only connector for your accounts in the Marketplace, let me know how it goes.
