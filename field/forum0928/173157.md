Hello,
It would be very helpful to be able to temporarily turn on and off Grok Bots access to idual oauth accounts in the settings
For example, as a user, I might want to turn off individual Gmail, google drive, google calendar, microsoft equivalent product, and finance plaid connected accounts so that my bots cannot access them at certain times. This would increase security of the app. When you want your bots to perform a task with sensitive data you could enable it temporarily, then disable it after.
We don’t have to remove the accounts, we just disable the bots access to the oauth tokens.
