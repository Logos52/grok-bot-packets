## Post #4 @deanrie (2026-09-23T04:16:26.909Z)
Hey @aliciar , thanks for the detailed report. This isn’t your network or your Mac. Your Grok Bot computer didn’t finish the very first startup, and in that state Reset and Update from the app can’t complete. Please don’t click Reset or Update for now. They won’t work in this state. Your account and the bots you created are safe. This is a known issue and we’re fixing it on our side. I’ve passed your case to the team so they can rebuild the computer. We’ll reply here as soon as it’s ready. After that, you’ll need to fully quit Grok Bot from the menu bar icon, then open it again. The fact that bot names and settings don’t sync between Mac and iPhone is most likely caused by the same thing. Those changes sync through the computer. Once the computer is back up, let me know if the names still don’t match across your devices.
