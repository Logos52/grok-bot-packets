### One paragraph
Host traces feudal land, enclosures, and Marx to one unanswered question: how to know what to make. Capital is a critique with no operating system. The capitalist asks about losses; the socialist calls losses failed exploitation. Berlin is the test: 2.7 million left East Germany by 1961; West same-day cars and 7,000–15,000 grocery items versus East's 3,000-item store, a 10-year Trabant wait, and a two-and-a-half to three year life-expectancy gap that closed 1989–1999. Nordic states, he says, are taxed free markets, not government-owned production. A center that allocates calories and medicine concentrates power until no one reports failure. The needle stays between collective security and individual freedom.
