## Recap · daily noon · 2026-09-22 (Asia/Taipei)

- All-In Summit with Naveen Rao of Unconventional AI: AI hits an energy wall in about three years unless hardware gets roughly a thousand times more efficient.
- Google alone already burns on the order of twelve gigawatts worth of tokens; about half of every served-token cost is energy, so power contracts alone will not close the gap.
- His fix is a dynamical 4D computer that unifies compute and memory instead of shuttling bits the von Neumann way — first physical chip taped out June 1, generating images at nanojoule scale.
- Company goal is to beat biology on intelligence per watt within roughly three and a half years, then push compute into many small local data centers and robot swarms.
- Chamath Q and A: about two years to a tokens-in tokens-out data-center rack; existing models port at the model layer; team bridges dynamical-systems theorists and chip builders with Python libraries.
- Other pins quiet this pass — Maxinomics, Justin Sung, Fern, Moon, Frontpage, and Elon sit-downs have nothing new.
