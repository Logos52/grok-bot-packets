Press Center The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt — Songs of the Past at gamescom 2026 - CD PROJEKT RED Press Center News Projects Media en Sign In The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt — Songs of the Past at gamescom 2026 July 21st, 2026 CD PROJEKT RED is delighted to announce its presence at gamescom 2026, with new information on The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt — Songs of the Past being revealed during gamescom Opening Night Live. Snapshot CD PROJEKT RED will also showcase The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt — Songs of the Past on the gamescom show floor Showcases will be available in the business area from August 26 to August 28 Showcases will be available in the entertainment area from August 26 to August 30 Hosted by Geoff Keighley, gamescom Opening Night Live is a premiere showcase and key date on the gaming calendar, featuring exclusive trailers, announcements, and more. Additionally, players and press attending gamescom in Cologne, Germany can receive a guided presentation of Songs of the Past at the CD PROJEKT RED booth. Showcases of the brand new expansion will be held in the business area, booth B065 in Hall 4.2, from August 26 to August 28, and in the entertainment area, booths B030 and A031 in Hall 8.1, from August 26 to August 30. Songs of the Past will return players to the role of legendary monster slayer Geralt of Rivia when it launches in 2027 on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC. It adds a new chapter to The Witcher 3 , a game that has sold more than 65 million copies since its release in 2015 and is the winner of more than 250 Game of the Year awards and 1,000 industry awards. More information about The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt can be found on its official website as well as Facebook , Instagram , and X .
