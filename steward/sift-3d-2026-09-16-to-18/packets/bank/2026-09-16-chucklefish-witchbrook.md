FAQ About Witchbrook Welcome to Mossport, a vibrant seaside town, where Witchbrook College opens its doors to aspiring witches like you! Step into a world where the familiar and the fantastical entwine, as you learn to harness the magic around you in this richly detailed life-sim and social RPG. Hop on your broom and dive into Mossport; a town bursting with enchantment, forgotten secrets, spells, and mystical spirits! Make lasting friendships, find love, and help others chase their dreams as you embark on the path to graduation and beyond. Who is developing Witchbrook? Witchbrook is being developed by London-based indie developer and publisher, Chucklefish ! More recently, they have been joined in co-development by the talented team at Robotality . When can we play? We have recently announced that Witchbrook will be coming to PC, Nintendo Switch, and Xbox in 2026. Chucklefish are proud to be a zero-crunch studio. This does mean that development has taken slightly longer than some people may be used to, but we truly believe that developers who are healthy and well-rested make better games! Where can I play? Witchbrook will be coming to Steam , Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and Xbox . Other platforms TBD! Will you have x language?
