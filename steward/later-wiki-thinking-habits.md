## Habit checklist (for wiki drafting)
1. **Start at the end** — lead with the thesis / answer, then the rationale.
2. **Observations before conclusions** — evidence first; keep conclusions provisional.
3. **Clarify the question** — name the crux / split the ask before writing.
4. **State the antithesis** — strongest counters next to the claim.
5. **Keep it concise** — compress to a one–two liner, then expand without fluff.
