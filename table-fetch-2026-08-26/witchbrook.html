FAQ

About Witchbrook

Welcome to Mossport, a vibrant seaside town, where Witchbrook College opens its doors to aspiring witches like you!

Step into a world where the familiar and the fantastical entwine, as you learn to harness the magic around you in this richly detailed life-sim and social RPG. Hop on your broom and dive into Mossport; a town bursting with enchantment, forgotten secrets, spells, and mystical spirits! Make lasting friendships, find love, and help others chase their dreams as you embark on the path to graduation and beyond.

Who is developing Witchbrook?

Witchbrook is being developed by London-based indie developer and publisher, Chucklefish! More recently, they have been joined in co-development by the talented team at Robotality.

When can we play?

We have recently announced that Witchbrook will be coming to PC, Nintendo Switch, and Xbox in 2026.

Chucklefish are proud to be a zero-crunch studio. This does mean that development has taken slightly longer than some people may be used to, but we truly believe that developers who are healthy and well-rested make better games!

Where can I play?

Witchbrook will be coming to Steam, Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and Xbox. Other platforms TBD!

Will you have x language?

Witchbrook will be available in English, French, German, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese.

Where can I go for updates?

We have a lovely Witchbrook community on our Chucklefish Discord server - join the conversation here! You can also follow us on Instagram, TikTok, Bluesky, and X where we’ll be posting about Witchbrook and our other games. Oh, and don’t forget to sign up to the college’s very own Witchbrook Oracle newspaper!

I’m a creator, can I sign up for updates?

We’re not quite ready to give access to creators just yet, but you are welcome to sign up here with your information, and we’ll assess from there. We typically don’t make judgements based on numbers alone, but love to work with people with engaged and passionate communities.

What if I want to cover Witchbrook now?

Please feel free to use the assets in our press kit to support your content. You are welcome to monetise content on Witchbrook under fair use (let’s play, commentary, fan art, cosplay etc.) so long as the works are transformative and don’t break copyright law or platform terms of service.
