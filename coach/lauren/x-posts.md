## D. (a) Deduplicated operator advice from Lauren, by theme
Attribution tags: **[her text]** = direct words from a post/article; **[her voice, transcript]** = podcast captions; **[2nd-hand]** = third-party summary of her talk. Items marked * are extrapolations of mine, flagged as such.

### Routines / cost
- **Don't schedule too often.** "a 15 min routine runs almost 100 times a day, and every run consumes tokens. hourly or a few times a day is usually good enough." [her text, Aug 22]
- **Long chats make routines expensive.** Give recurring routines to a *fresh* bot; keep your main chat (chief of staff) separate. [her text, Aug 22]
- **Audit routines regularly.** Dr Eggbot v0.2.0: weekly routine health check for "unused or expensive" routines; also asks you at install. [her text, Sep 1] Her own rule of thumb: "every 10 minutes… a lot of wakeups". [her voice]
- **Her own X-monitor routine runs about every 30 minutes** and aggregates duplicates before she looks. [her voice] (Note: that's on the tighter side of her own advice — she's a heavy user.)
- **Save the heavy toolkit for hard tasks.** * (Ray Fernando's 2B-token day when he pstack'd everything is his account, not her advice; consistent with "use for hard tasks".)
- Product-side context: the team said it was "working on efficiency improvements" (Aug 24) — her post quotes Jediah asking for agent IDs.
- Adjacent, from Eric Z.'s curated thread (not hers): event triggers over cron, connectors over browser clicking, delete one-shot watches. Ben Lang thread: reverse-engineer expensive browser tasks into scripts (Sam Sokolin), webhooks as triggers.

### Skills
- **Do the task manually once, watch, correct, then make it a skill; only when it one-shots, make it a routine.** [her voice; Peter Yang's paraphrase]
- **Point the bot at where the skills live** — put the repo path in the bot's description. [her text, reply R1, Aug 25]
- **Invest in a verification skill first**: `/create-verification-skill` → "teaches your agent how to run, control, and debug your app"; creates a Feature Map "of all the features in your app and how to get to it… from a user's pov". Keep it fresh with a daily `/maintain-verification-skill` (via Grok Bot routine or Cursor automation). [her text, Jul 30, Aug 28, Aug 31]
- **"Build the Lever"**: prefer giving agents a small CLI/tool over prose instructions — fewer tokens, more reproducible. CLI should have `--dry-run` for anything destructive, descriptive errors, JSON output. [her text, pstack Pt.1]
- Skills as evals: pstack is maintained by having sub-agents on several models try prompts and only landing a change if the goal is met. [her voice; 2nd-hand]
- Interop: plugins with skills work in both Cursor and Grok Bot; invoke with `/poteto-mode`. [her voice; her text]

### Delegation / architecture of bots
- **Use bots as coordinators; have cloud agents do the coding.** "spawn a cloud agent to use /poteto-mode to build…" — frees the bot and keeps its context clean. [her text, Pt.1] (Eric Z. gives the same tip.)
- **Eng-lead bot that never does the work**: description holds the bot IDs of the (four) eng bots; "never do work on its own and always delegate". Talk to a chief of staff, who talks to the eng lead. Created via Dr Eggbot. [her voice]
- **Split bots by purpose, name them.** One agent doing too many things accumulates confusing context. [her voice] Bots named Daifuku/Gyoza/Matcha/Crumb/Katsu (Peter's post). Think of it like hiring/training employees. [her voice]
- **Outer loop → inner loop**: routines that "farm context" (Slack bug reports, X complaints, feature ideas) feed a bot she then tells "go talk to potato… come back with a design proposal" — no copy-paste. [her text; her voice]
- **Team Bots (Sep 28)**: shared bot with role-scoped skills/plugins/credentials, added to Slack. [her text]
- Full Autopilot: `/goal`, `/loop`, `/swarm` to make agents "fully own, verify, and ship". [her text] `/swarm` fans out many cloud agents to run verification (e.g., perf win sample size, fuzzing). [her text, Pt.1]
- Prefer cloud agents over git worktrees for parallelism; worktrees ≈ ~10 agents max on a good machine. [her text]
- Supervising someone smarter: state intent, don't over-specify; let the agent restate the problem in its own words; `/recall` past chats. [her text, Pt.2]

### Verification
- "Verification means that an agent can verify its own work. It can keep going until it succeeds… without you being the bottleneck." [her text, Pt.1] "You remain the bottleneck, and your whole day will be spent babysitting your agents." [Pt.2]
- Have it prove work with evidence: "use /control-app to verify your changes and show me a video and screenshots as proof". [her text]
- Agents "state things confidently without backing it up" — make them show data / explain via `/teach`. [her text, Pt.2]
- Verify on real environments incl. your own machine (VM via local-exec daemon). [her voice, podcast]
- Mechanical guardrails over reminders (lint/CI, hard boundaries, ban footguns like useEffect). [her text (Dune ban); 2nd-hand for CI details]
- Ordering: build/verify locally first, then scale to cloud; "trust curve" from watching 1 agent to 10–20. [2nd-hand]
- Honest counterpoint: the AI Socratic write-up notes PR counts prove delivery, not quality; and it's self-reported.

### Safety / approval
- Keep a human approval on irreversible/external actions: virtual card — "You approve every request." [her text, Aug 28]; inline drafts of Slack/email for approval before sending [@bot post she quoted, Sep 9; Peng in podcast]; trip booking: "check in with me before you hit book" [her voice]. Reversibility as the line: draft-first [2nd-hand].
- Trust is gradual: earn it by observing, then skills, then routines; walking away comes last. [her voice]
- I found **no** post by her giving secrets-handling or permissions-mode advice (Always Allow/Auto Review, secrets field) — those items in docs/Ben Lang's list are from the team/docs, not her.
- Caveat on her own autonomy level: she lets agents merge their own PRs and reviews after landing — but that is backed by Dune's guardrails + verification infra she built; don't copy the autonomy without the infra.

### pstack
- Install the plugin in Cursor or Grok Bot; start prompts with `/poteto-mode` (router to playbooks; "maximum impact with the least amount of code"). [her text; LinkedIn snippet]
- In Cursor, Opt+Enter on the autocompleted `/poteto-mode` pins it as a Custom Mode (reminder every turn). [her text, Pt.1]
- Run `/setup-pstack` to choose models (writes model routing rules — SNIPPET only). [her text, Aug 28]
