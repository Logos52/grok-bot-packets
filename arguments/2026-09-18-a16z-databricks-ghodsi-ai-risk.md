### One paragraph
Martin Casado and Sarah Wang host Databricks CEO Ali Ghodsi for a long argument about AI risk that deliberately separates near-zero existential scare talk from real cyber/agent risk and from the enterprise adoption gap. Ali opens that leaders should not blast humanity-wipeout odds to the public when existential risk is “close to zero,” while Martin adds that the scare has already reached rural prepper relatives and pause-curious federal politics (Warren, Bernie×Bannon, Abbott/data centers) — a blowback that can work against intended safety goals.
