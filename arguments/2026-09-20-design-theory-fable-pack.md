### One paragraph
John Mauriello teaches form-follows-function as a constraint craft: a pizza box only becomes designable once size, compostable molded fiber, and stacking loads are specified, echoing Louis Sullivan’s late-1800s dictum. He insists projects start with written design criteria so no color or curve is arbitrary and cross-functional audiences hear goals not vibes; research (*Designing for Growth*) scopes the problem. Function is then made legible through visual indicators (Braun key, cockpit shapes), foolproofing (USB-C), sensible mapping (stove knobs — via Norman), and honest manufacturing choices that change both look and possibility. Finally he warns that convention and MAYA temper radical “better” layouts (Porsche’s top-trigger drill): know the status quo and navigate pushback without becoming its prisoner.
