### Closing
Erik Torenberg hosts Replit CEO Amjad Masad with Horowitz Andreessen Academy CEO Gagan Biyani for a follow-on education conversation that centers *what young people are for* rather than restating the academy launch deck. Amjad’s through-line is heresy: *City and the Stars* as the parable for clear minds that ask what’s outside the monoculture, against colleges that beat outside thought into job-citizen compliance.
