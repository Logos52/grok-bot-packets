Charm classics (Enchanter — separate tier)

  2. #2
    Warrior / Cleric / Enchanter Unkillable tank with best healer, Enchanter charm turns any mob into your highest DPS dealer
  3. #3
    Monk / Shaman / Enchanter Monk DPS plus charm DPS, Shaman keeps everyone alive — fast and lethal
  4. #4
    Magician / Shaman / Enchanter Double pet plus charm, Shaman slow keeps charm pet manageable
  5. #5
    Paladin / Druid / Enchanter Self-sustaining trio — Paladin tanks and heals, Druid heals and ports, Enchanter charm and CC
