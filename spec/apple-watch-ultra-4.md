## 0. Does it exist? Yes.
- **Name:** Apple Watch Ultra 4 (also "Hermès Ultra 4"). Announced **9 Sep 2026** ("Surprise and Shine" event). Pre-order 9 Sep, **in stores 18 Sep 2026**. [READ: Apple newsroom; MacRumors/Verge snippets]
- **Design:** Identical to Ultra 3. 49 x 44 x 12 mm, Grade 5 titanium, flat sapphire, 3,000-nit LTPO3 OLED, 100 m water resistance (40 m dive), Action button, dual-frequency GPS, satellite SOS. Fits 130–210 mm wrists. [READ: Apple newsroom, Apple Store page; UK spec snippet]
- **What's new:**
  - S11 chip.
  - New "Health Sensing System": heart rate every 5 s all day, HRV up to 24x more often, readiness score 0–10.
  - Battery up to 50 h normal (was 42 h), 84 h Low Power Mode, 25 h Extended Workout, 45 h Max Extended Workout. 15 min of charging gives up to 18 h.
  - Audio Intelligence: Shazam and Sound Recognition now; Live Rewind and Siri Recap in beta "later this year". Siri AI on watchOS 27.
  - Redesigned Health app (Longevity tab, Health Age) "later this year". [READ: Apple newsroom]
- **Weight:** 63.0 g natural, 63.1 g black (UK spec snippet). AppleInsider says about +1.4 g vs Ultra 3 [READ]. Ultra 3 figures vary by source (61–61.6 g).
- **Cases:** **Natural titanium** and **Black titanium** only. No new case colour. [READ]
- **Bands (Apple 2026 lineup):** [READ: Apple newsroom + Apple Store page]
  - Trail Loop: Dark Umber, Sand, Burgundy.
  - Alpine Loop: Desert, Dark Olive, Burgundy.
  - Ocean Band: now translucent silicone. Translucent Gray, Kelp, Black.
  - Titanium Milanese Loop: natural and black.
  - **No black or gray Trail or Alpine in the 2026 lineup.**
- **Hermès Ultra 4:** natural titanium only. New Grand H titanium band. US from $1,499 (En Mer / Scub'H), $3,149 with Grand H [SNIPPET: Verge]. One blog said $2,199 for the Grand H band alone. Not relevant to his budget.
- **US price:** **from $799** (same as Ultra 3). Apple's own Leasing example: $24.99/mo for 24 months. [READ: Apple newsroom]
  - Apple has not published separate band prices, and its page shows no Milanese surcharge. [READ: MacObserver, 14 Sep]
  - Historic Apple prices (not confirmed for 2026):

    - Titanium Milanese $199 (AppleInsider, Feb 2025 [READ]).

## 3. Recommendations

### Case finish
- **Choose Black titanium** *if* you accept that scratches will show, or you add a bezel-covering protector. Why:
  - It suits a quiet black/gray wardrobe. A Japanese owner (けこぜろ) and aBlogtoWatch like the look.
  - Owner durability reports are mostly good: MacRumors and aBlogtoWatch say the DLC coating is tough.
- **Choose Natural titanium** if any visible mark would bother you. Reddit regret threads consistently say natural hides wear.
- **My pick for Wedge: Black.** Travel with the watch in bags or on desks could cause marks; a bezel-covering protector fixes it. If you'd rather not wear a protector, choose Natural.

### Band type by use (wrist size unknown — measure it)
- Measure wrist circumference with a soft tape at the wrist bone, in mm. The Ultra fits 130–210 mm. Sizes: Milanese S 130–160, M 155–185, L 180–210 mm [AppleInsider].
- Why it matters: Ultra 4's heart-rate and readiness features need tight contact (Tom's Guide). Thin wrists may need a snug Trail Loop.
- Ranking for daily wear, sleep and travel:
  1. **Trail Loop**: lightest, softest, fully adjustable. Velcro wears over time (Japanese owner).
  2. **Titanium Milanese (black)**: best premium/dressy look, no hair-pulling per owners. Fiddly clasp, scratches laptops, some find it stiff or returned it. Cost: extra Apple charge; in VN about +3,000,000 ₫ over the base price.
  3. **Ocean (translucent)**: only for water, gym and sweat. Silicone isn't breathable (Verge). Translucent variants are brand-new, so I found no long-term owner reports.
  4. **Alpine**: secure and good for hiking, but the hook is a chore to take on and off (Verge), so poor fit for the quiet, daily-use brief.

### Band colour
- **Japanese-minimal, black/gray:**
  - Titanium Milanese in black, to match the black case.
  - Translucent Black or Gray Ocean.
  - Trail Loop **Dark Umber** (deep brown, the quietest of the three Trail colours). Sand is light; Burgundy is loud.
- Alpine Dark Olive is the quiet option in that line. Avoid Desert and Burgundy.
- Apple's 2026 lineup has no black/gray Trail or Alpine. The previous-year black Trail may still be around from older stock. I did not confirm that.

### Third-party bands (sentiment only)
- **Nomad Rocky Point** (FKM rubber, titanium hardware, black or natural hardware available). Owner snippets: many find it more comfortable than Apple's Ocean, rugged, flexible. Complaints: stiff, bulky for sleep and typing, lug fit wobble, matte black finish can scratch. Nomad states Ultra / 46 mm size; boxes may still say Ultra 1–3. [SNIPPET only]
- **Nomad titanium band:** beautiful quality but the install is difficult and lugs can scratch the case (Reddit). [SNIPPET only]
- **Pitaka** aramid/carbon link band: reviewers praise light weight and a premium link look; sizing adjustment is fiddly. Prices in snippets: $99 standard, $119 Rhapsody. [SNIPPET; a Techaeris review is also affiliate-linked]
- **Rubber B:** makes straps for mechanical watches such as Rolex and Omega, not for Apple Watch. I found nothing for Ultra.
- **Hirsch, Casetify:** no reliable Ultra-specific owner sentiment found. Not recommended on evidence.
- Third-party caveats: the accessorytested.com page that lists Nomad, Spigen and MoKo rankings carries Amazon links and invented-looking review counts, so I excluded its ratings.
- Use Spigen Lite Fit Ultra or similar only if you want a cheap sleep band; I have no owner evidence for it.

**Buy now or wait?**
- **Buy now** if you want an Ultra. First reviewers (AppleInsider 4/5, Tom's Guide "best Ultra yet", Men's Health) all say a first-time Ultra buyer should take the Ultra 4. I found no verified Ultra redesign rumour. The design is the same, so there is nothing design-based to wait for.
- **Wait for software, not hardware:** Health app redesign, Live Rewind and Siri Recap, and Siri AI in more languages all arrive later.
- **Skip the Ultra** if 49 mm feels too big on your wrist. Reviewers point to the **Series 12** for small wrists. Try one on in person, especially in the US.
- **Upgrading from an Ultra 3 is not recommended** by reviewers unless you want the battery and heart-rate gains.

Caveats: Reddit threads were blocked from the box, so Reddit findings are from search snippets. No named Japanese-minimalist creator on your list has an Ultra verdict I could find.
