# Free Apple Watch apps worth having on an Ultra 4 (watchOS 27)
Researched 30 Sep 2026 for Wedge. US App Store. Ratings and prices are from the live iTunes lookup and App Store pages today.

**Every third-party app below has Apple Watch in its US App Store device list.** I checked the iTunes lookup device list, and for some also the page text.
**Vietnam:** every app below returns a listing in the Vietnam App Store (iTunes lookup, country=vn, all "Free"). That shows the app is downloadable there. It does not show that its data or features work in Vietnam. I note that separately where it matters.
**Sponsored picks:** none. I took no paid or affiliate lists as a source.

## Honourable mentions (free-ish, not in the 12)
- **Zones for Training** (4.78★ / 19K). Free: real-time heart-rate zones with a wrist tap on zone changes. PRO ($7.99) unlocks stats and custom zones.
- **Seconds Interval Timer** (4.69★ / 23.7K). Free to try, with a catch: each timer locks after one run until you pay a one-time $7.99 unlock. Seconds Pro is the same app for $7.99 up front. No Smart Stack widget or complication documented.
- **Overcast** (4.54★ / 47.9K). Free podcast app with a standalone watch app, funded by ads. Premium ($19.99–$29.99/yr) hides the ads.

## Checks that changed the picture
- **Google Translate has no watch app.** Its listing shows iPhone and iPad only.
- **Apple Translate** is 2.35★ and about 10K ratings (earlier file). Live Translation in Messages supports Vietnamese on supported watches.
- **Tally Quick Counter** (4.66★ / 1.8K): the watch app is a Tally Pro feature, so it is not free.
- **Pacer Pedometer** (4.90★ / 332K): the watch app is on the store, and the core tracking, groups and many challenges are free. Premium adds an AI coach, guided workouts and Garmin sync. Complications are unclear: a 2019 Pacer blog describes one, but a search summary says the current app does not offer one. I excluded it.
- **Dictation:** Vietnamese dictation, Siri Speaks and the Vietnamese keyboard are on Apple's watchOS 27 availability page for Vietnam. Siri itself does not list Vietnamese.
- **Apple Maps** has no turn-by-turn navigation for Vietnam. That is why Google Maps matters.
- **Grab, Be, Xanh SM, Zalo:** no watch app in their App Store listings. (Earlier file confirms Grab, Be and Xanh SM, and I re-checked Grab and Zalo.)
