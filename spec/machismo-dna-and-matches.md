# Machismo DNA and brands that actually match (for Wedge)

Written 29 Sep 2026. Wedge: 172 cm, lives in Vietnam, visits the US. Budget: machismo prices or lower.
This file does not repeat the first pass (`similar-brands-machismo.md`). It goes back to machismo's own catalog and images, then tests candidates against that.

**Currency:** about 31 TWD per USD and about 25,500 VND per USD (my approximations).
**Evidence labels used below:**
- **[IMG]** = I downloaded the product photos and looked at them.
- **[TXT]** = product page text only.
- **[SNIP]** = search-result summary only. I did not open the source.

**How I "looked" at images.** I built 4-up and 5-up contact sheets and read them with an image-description tool. The tool labelled every sheet "Machismo", including other brands' photos, so I attributed each image by its source URL and ignored the tool's brand labels. Fine details such as stitching and fabric hand are less reliable than silhouette, palette and layout.

### 1.3 What the pictures show [IMG]
Reading the 64 photos I viewed:
- **Tops:** boxy, short, drop-shouldered, with wide elbow-length sleeves.
- **Trousers:** high-waisted, wide or cocoon, with double front pleats, side waist tabs or elastic-plus-cord waists, and cropped ankle length.
- **Palette:** navy, black, olive, khaki, tan, grey and slate.
- **Details, not prints:** braided cord ties, contrast piping, offset zips, side gill-pleats, oversized patch pockets and webbing straps.
- **Knits:** bouclé and heathered half-zips, cable or braided zip trim, and a colour-blocked "detachable sleeve" knit.

### 1.4 THE MACHISMO DNA (top 10 traits, with evidence)

1. **Short, boxy, drop-shoulder tops with wide sleeves that reach the elbow.**
   - Images: printed blouse, Delave tees, Tencel U-collar top, eco-blue six-sleeve top and sea-wave pleated top all show short length, low shoulders and wide sleeves.
   - Text: "短身版型，拉高身形" (short cut, lengthens the figure) on the tactical vest and the leaf-jacquard shirt, and "寬身微短版型 (Crop Cut)" on the shirt.
   - Measured: printed blouse chest 60–62 cm, length 71–72 cm.
2. **High-waist wide, cocoon or tapered trousers with pleats and an adjustable waist.**
   - Products: Hakama wide pants, drawstring tapered pants (上寬下窄, "wide top, narrow bottom"), large-pocket cocoon trousers (free size, 30–40" waist), Shamilan straight wide pants, washed-look straight pants (deep pleat).
   - Images show pleats plus elastic or tab waists on all of them.
3. **Japanese-clothing forms turned into modern basics.** Haori, noragi, hakama, dōfuku-style collarless layers, with cord ties.
   - Products: baseball-jersey haori, Body Shell Dry long haori (double cord plus a mizuhiki knot at the back), noragi blazer, Hakama pants, "上指糸" cord waist, snow-fox collarless wool coat.
   - This is the most distinctive trait. Few competitors do it.
4. **Japanese technical or "tech-natural" fabrics used for drape and easy care, not for outdoor performance.**
   - Named in the descriptions: TORAY Body Shell Dry, Shamilan (linen-look), Evalet (linen-like), ECO BLUE (recycled PET), vertical® Dobby, Tencel blends, typewriter cloth.
   - The stated selling points are wrinkle-resistance, quick-dry, machine-washable and breathable.
   - The hand aims for "looks like linen or cotton, behaves like polyester". A synthetic fibre (polyester, nylon, rayon or acrylic) is named in 34 of 40 items.
5. **Muted, low-saturation palette.**
   - Navy, black, olive, 德軍灰 (German-army grey), khaki, mocha, latte.
   - Text says 低彩度 (low saturation) on four items.
   - The images agree, with a few exceptions (Delave terracotta, a tricolour loop-knit, a leaf-print).
6. **Utility hardware and pockets, kept clean.**
   - Products: Fidlock magnetic buckle and Duraflex D-ring, YKK zips, big patch pockets, tactical vest with webbing and loops, M47 military work pants, MA-1 with a detachable collar.
   - Images: webbing belt with D-ring, big patch pockets, nothing shiny or logo-heavy.
7. **Layering pieces as a system.** Vests, haori and half-open knits are built to sit over tees and under coats.
   - "層次" appears in 17 descriptions.
   - Examples: the half-open turtleneck wool knit (zips open as a light jacket or closed as an inner layer), the tactical vest that buttons to an M-65 parka, the detachable-sleeve knit, the Delave 3-way tee.
8. **Small hidden design tricks instead of graphics.**
   - Products: gill-pleat tops and trousers (鰓裂), folded-line tee, sea-wave pleated top (摺景), fishnet-mesh side or back panels, off-centre or asymmetric closures, contrast piping.
   - Images confirm side louvre pleats, offset zips and mesh panels.
9. **Knits that are textured and cosy, not fine.**
   - Products: bouclé (acrylic 75 / wool 25) half-zip, 92% wool half-open turtleneck with braided trim and a hem cord, waveknit (acrylic 86 / wool 9 / polyester 5), detachable-sleeve wool loop knit (wool 67 / polyester 33), tricolour loop-knit.
   - Images: fuzzy or bouclé surfaces, heathered colour-block, braided seam trim.
10. **Made in Taiwan, mostly free-size or M/L cuts, at a fairly accessible price.**
    - "Japanese A-grade fabric selected / Taiwan department-store-counter team" on every page.
    - Free-size or M/L only.
    - NT$2,108–3,980 for almost everything (about US$68–128).

**Where machismo is *not* minimal.** A few loud items break the quiet rule: the printed blouse, the tricolour loop-knit and the Delave terracotta tee. The images also show one bright orange pop in some outfits.
