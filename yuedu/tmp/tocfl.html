提醒考生提高警覺

成績單與證書可透過教育部驗證系統查驗。請勿使用假文件或委託他人代辦報名，以免受騙並影響升學、居留或工作權益。詳情請見最新公告。

數位成績單與證書，自2026年1月1日全面上線

2026年1月1日起的海內外華語文能力測驗，將改採數位成績單與證書，不再核發紙本成績單與證書。
詳情請見最新公告。

Important Notice
Important Notice for TOCFL Test Takers

TOCFL transcripts and certificates can be verified through the Ministry of Education's Digital Certificate Verification System. Please use official TOCFL channels for registration and document verification to avoid potential risks that may affect your rights related to school admission, residency, or employment. For more details, please see theLatest News.

Digital Score Reports and Certificates Available from January 1, 2026

From January 1, 2026, all TOCFL tests in Taiwan and overseas will use digital score reports and certificates only. Paper score reports and certificates will no longer be issued. For more details, please see the Latest News.
