  • Regarding Season 1 of Crucible Rankings

    -

    Since the release of beastmaster, we've seen many players enjoying the new limited job and tackling its challenges. That said, we have also received considerable feedback on beastmaster's combat balance, as well as reports of issues with certain gameplay mechanics, which we are working to address.

    If rankings were to begin under these conditions, it would be difficult to determine whether the results reflect the intended balance of the job and its mechanics, impacting the fairness of the ranking process.

    For these reasons, we have made the difficult decision of postponing the start of Crucible Rankings.

    We are working to resolve issues and address feedback as a top priority, and an announcement will be made when a date for the start of Season 1 rankings has been decided.

    * Please be advised that Crucible Ranking scores will remain unavailable in-game until a starting date has been decided.

  • Rolling Out Same-region Player Matching for the Japan Data Center!

    -

    As announced at Fan Festival 2026 in Berlin, starting Tuesday, October 13, 2026, following the 24 hour maintenance period on Monday, October 12, same-region player matching will be available for the Japan data center.

    Matchmaking and party recruitment have thus far been limited to players on the same logical data center. However, with the introduction of same-region player matching, the scope will expand to cover all those on the same physical data center. This means that all players within the same physical data center will be eligible for matchmaking and party recruitment, regardless of their current world.

    Once same-region player matching has been confirmed to operate stably on the Japan data center, it will be rolled out in turn to other data centers.

    This announcement will outline the purpose and impact of same-region player matching.

    Read on for details.

    * Updated on Wednesday, September 16, 2026 at 4:00 a.m. (PDT)
