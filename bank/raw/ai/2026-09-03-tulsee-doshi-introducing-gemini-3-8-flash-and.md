Google announced Gemini 3.8 Flash and Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber on September 2, 2026. The standard model is positioned as a low-cost workhorse for long-horizon coding and autonomous agents, while Cyber is offered to trusted defenders through the Fairwind Program. Google reports 54.9% on HLE-Verified, more than 70% success on its internal multi-language vulnerability benchmark, and 47.2% pass@1 on CWE-Bench. The company says Chrome Security found 2.6 times more correct patches than larger commercial models, and Cloud Vulnerability Research found a critical foundational vulnerability in under two hours. The introductory price is $0.75 per million input tokens and $3.75 per million output tokens through December 31, 2026.
