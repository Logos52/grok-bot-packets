Right now the plugin page shows one account row and one tool count for the whole plugin, and both reflect only its first connector (the docs one, which needs no sign-in). To finish it: On iPhone, open Plugins and tap Cloudflare. Tap Retry. Complete the Cloudflare sign-in in the browser that opens and approve access. When you return to the app, check whether the “didn’t finish connecting” line is gone. Could you reply with whether that clears it, and whether bindings shows up for your Bot afterwards?
