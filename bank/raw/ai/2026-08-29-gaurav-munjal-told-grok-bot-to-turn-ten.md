I cannot ever go back to a world without AI Agents. It's like we are living in a world with Magic.

Told Grok Bot to go through my Calendar for the last ten years, each meeting. And then each person whom I met add them on a database in Notion with their LinkedIn Profiles and what they are up to currently. With proper tags etc.

It's been 2.5 hours since the Agent is working without any break. So far it has done the task for last three years and added about 1000 entries to the database.

Catalog (grokbot.dev, reconstructed prompt, not verbatim): Rolodex / relationship archivist. Create Notion People DB. Work backwards through calendar. Skip blocks/holds/self. For each person: how we met, first/last met, LinkedIn, current role, tags, Confidence. If not confident, guess in field, set Confidence low, flag for review — never present a guess as fact. Work in batches and checkpoint. Never invent a person, company, or LinkedIn. Private network data — do not share. After each batch: how many added, how many need review.
