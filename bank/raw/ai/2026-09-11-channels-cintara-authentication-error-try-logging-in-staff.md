## @deanrie 2026-09-10T18:30:26 [STAFF]

Hey @Channels_Cintara. I can see the screenshot with the banner: “Authentication error / If you are logged in, try logging out and back in”.

Your session is fine. There’s nothing else you need to do on the login side, and reinstalling won’t help. Signing out and back in won’t fix this one. This isn’t caused by your setup. The message is showing up because of a recent change on our side that’s preventing some Grok Bot computers from reaching the model. I’ve already flagged it to the team and we’re tracking it.

If you don’t want to wait for the fix, you can update Grok Bot’s Computer to a newer build that isn’t affected:

- Open Grok Bot.
- Go to Settings, then Grok Bot’s Computer.
- Click Update and wait for it to finish. Your bots and files will stay in place.
- Send a test message to the bot.

If Update doesn’t work, or if the error is still there after step 4, reply here and I’ll reset your Grok Bot machine from my side. I’ll keep your bots and data. I’ll also update the thread as soon as we have news on the fix.
