@kevinn · 2026-09-30T23:40:56
Hey @Mitra , thanks for the report, and sorry for the slow follow-up on your email. Good news first: your 12 publishing Bots have not been deleted. They are still on your account. They’re hidden right now because the account no longer has active Grok Bot access, so the app can’t load them or reach Grok Bot’s computer. Signing into the second account didn’t remove them. It only cleared the list the app had saved on your device, and the app can’t reload that list without access. About the usage: the Grok Bot Galaxy credit is a fixed amount of free usage, not unlimited use for a period of time. Twelve Bots training at once used all of it by about 9:50 PM (Toronto time) on Sept 23. That credit is tracked separately from the Weekly Usage bar, which is why the bar stayed at 0% the whole time. I can see how that’s confusing. To get your Bots back, the account needs Grok Bot access again. You can use Start Trial in the app, a paid plan that includes Grok Bot, or link a SuperGrok subscription. Once that’s active, fully quit and reopen Grok Bot with the same account, and your publishing Bots should show up again. Keep in mind that Bots working on their own overnight, or messaging each other, keep using usage the whole time. Until then, please don’t use Recover Grok Bot’s Computer or Reset, and don’t recreate the Bots with the same names. If anything is still missing once access is back, reply here and we’ll take a look.
