## Post #2 @kevinn (2026-09-24T18:02:02.386Z)
Hey @Ronnie_O , thanks for the suggestion! Right now Grok Bot has its own weekly allowance that’s separate from your Cursor plan usage, so leftover Grok Bot usage can’t be applied to Cursor models. Simplifying usage is something we are working on so stay tuned for updates here. In the meantime, if your Cursor usage runs out and you still have Grok Bot allowance left, you can hand coding tasks to Grok Bot and have it work on its own computer (instead of launching Cursor cloud agents). That work draws from your Grok Bot weekly allowance rather than your Cursor plan.
