Role-based learning helps employees, developers, leaders, educators, and students build practical AI skills and demonstrate what they’ve learned.

Choose a learning path for the work you doKnowledge workers: Apply AI at Work

Using AI safely and effectively takes practice. People need to learn how to apply it to the work in front of them, whether they’re improving a daily workflow, building a product, leading a team, or teaching a class.

At OpenAI, we treat learning as part of deployment. Today, we’re expanding OpenAI Academy with new courses for developers, leaders, educators, and college students. These join Apply AI at Work, which helps people build foundational skills, create repeatable workflows, and direct work with agents.

Together, the pathways help organizations build shared habits for working with AI and develop the skills different roles require. Course assessments give learners a way to demonstrate what they’ve learned. Learners earn an OpenAI Academy course badge by passing the course assessment.

Organizations can combine Apply AI at Work with focused learning for developers, leaders, educators, and students, choosing the courses that support their teams’ work.

As learners progress, they practice directing larger pieces of work with agents, deciding what to delegate and where to add checkpoints and human review. The pathway helps employees take on more complex tasks with AI while retaining responsibility for the final result.

These courses are designed to help organizations move from experimentation to useful, reliable AI systems.

Leaders must also decide where AI can create meaningful value, what the organization should prioritize, who owns the work, and how progress will be measured.

In AI for Educators, learners use materials they have permission to use to plan a class, create an activity or assessment, or adapt communications for students, teaching assistants, or colleagues. They review ChatGPT’s responses against their learning objectives, source materials, and requirements. The educator decides what belongs in their teaching.

AI for College Students focuses on academic and career tasks. Students practice organizing readings and deadlines into a study plan, assigning roles in a group project, reviewing a draft against assignment requirements, and preparing for applications or interviews. They learn to bring the right materials into ChatGPT, strengthen their work, and make decisions about the final result.

Learning is most useful when people have time to practice and apply it. Managers and Champions can encourage learners to bring real tasks into the courses, share useful examples, and connect what they learn to work already underway.

The courses are shaped by teams across OpenAI and will continue to be updated as our models, products, and guidance change.

New pathways include Build with AI (developers), Lead AI Adoption (leaders), and Teach and Learn with AI (educators and college students), joining Apply AI at Work.
