Hey @Todd_Cunningham , thanks for the report and for already trying the update and restart. A bit of background on how this works: Grok Bot’s own screenshot tool is view-only on purpose. When it needs to click, type or browse, it hands that step to a background computer-use helper that drives its desktop. That helper is still available to ToddBot (it’s working normally on your account), but ToddBot has stopped using it and is telling you the tool is gone. That’s also why updating the computer and restarting the app didn’t change anything. Could you try this and tell me what happens? Open the ToddBot chat in Grok Bot. Send exactly: “Launch a computer-use helper (Task tool, computerUse) to open https://www.google.com in your browser and click the search box. Don’t reply until it finishes.” Wait for it to finish without sending another message. If it works, you can ask ToddBot to always hand browser work to its computer-use helper. If it has saved a note or instruction saying it has no browser control, ask it to remove that note. If it still says it can’t, please reply with the time you tried (with your time zone) and ToddBot’s exact reply, and I’ll dig in from there.
