What it does

Morning and evening, the same question arrives from the children’s catechism written by Errol Hulse. The household has its go at replying first, and the set response follows once you have, so the repeating rhythm does the teaching instead of a lecture. Built for families who want that rhythm run for them. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
