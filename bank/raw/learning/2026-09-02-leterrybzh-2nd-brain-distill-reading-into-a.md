## What it does

Everything you feed it lands as raw material first, whether that is a PDF, a scribbled note or a post you bookmarked. It then writes tight, cross-linked entries and keeps that web of them tidy as the collection grows. Ask it something and the reply is drawn from your own entries. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
