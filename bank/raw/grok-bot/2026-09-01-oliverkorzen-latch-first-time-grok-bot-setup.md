What it does

Built for the moment right after signing up, when nothing is plugged in yet. It asks what you do and which software you already lean on, gets the relevant integrations authenticated for you, then puts them straight to work on a genuine errand before you close the tab, so setup ends with proof instead of a checklist. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @OliverKorzen shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
