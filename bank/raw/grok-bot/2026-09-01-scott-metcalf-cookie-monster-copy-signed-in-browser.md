What it does

When several Grok Bots share one machine and have to work inside signed-in websites, stale sessions quietly break them. This helper copies the correct per-origin browser session data across to the machine they share, so every bot in the roster starts already authenticated rather than stuck on a sign-in screen. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @scottxmetcalf shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
