Blizzard’s September Trading Post is themed around friendship and BlizzCon 2026. The monthly bonus reward is the Crested Ember Leafmimic mount, earned by filling the Traveler’s Log bar. The month’s inventory includes the Crested Verdant Leafmimic, Gummi pet, mounts, armor and weapon transmogs. Special Outlet vendors in Dornogal and Silvermoon City offer returning items, including previous monthly bonus rewards, for Trader’s Tender. Players with an active account receive 500 Trader’s Tender from the Collector’s Cache at the beginning of each month, and can earn up to 500 more through monthly activities.
