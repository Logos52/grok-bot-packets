  • Moogle Treasure Trove - The First Hunt for Astronomy Commences September 9!

    -

    It gives us great pleasure to announce the return of the Moogle Treasure Trove starting Wednesday, September 9, 2026!

    This Moogle Treasure Trove will be a special edition to celebrate the upcoming release of Evercold, taking part across two installments and with an even more exciting range of items, including a special reward: an uolon horn!

    As well as bestowing irregular tomestones as usual, the First Hunt for Astronomy will also reward those who complete minimog and ultimog challenges with a unique item required to obtain the uolon horn. Details can be found on the special site below.

    The First Hunt for Astronomy and the Second Hunt for Astronomy, to be held at a later date, will feature a different lineup of items and different tomestones required for exchange. However, please note that the uolon horn will be available in the Second Hunt as well.

    Visit the Moogle Treasure Trove - The Hunt for Astronomy special site.

    * Irregular tomestones of astronomy I will not be awarded when using adventurer squadrons or the Duty Support system.
    * Unrestricted parties must have the “Level Sync” option enabled to receive irregular tomestones of astronomy I.
